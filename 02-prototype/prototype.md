## What it demonstrates

Triaging customer feedback and enabling uploading and exporting the results within the tool. Summarizing and provides initial assessment of priority while also enabling the PM to update the results, i.e. change the prioritization or indicate a high-value customer. 

_____

## Debrief

- **What worked:** All relevant information is available while consolidating it in a way that is most useable to myself as a PM and cuts down not only the analysis but also the follow-on work after identifying priorities.
- **What broke / felt like a toy:** The sentiment aspect was not clear where the information was coming from or how it was being rated. 
- **What I'd change next pass:** Refine the look/feel so the most important information is more quickly seen and understood.
